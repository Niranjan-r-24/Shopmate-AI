================================================================================
SHOPMATE RETAIL - SHIPPING AND DELIVERY POLICY
================================================================================

1. SHIPPING TIERS AND DELIVERY TIMELINES
- Standard Ground Shipping (3 - 5 business days):
  FREE on all orders over $49.00. Flat rate of $5.99 for orders under $49.00.
- Expedited 2-Day Shipping (2 business days):
  $12.99 flat rate. Guaranteed delivery within 2 business days when ordered before 2:00 PM EST.
- Overnight Priority Express (1 business day):
  $24.99 flat rate. Next business day delivery if placed before 12:00 PM EST Monday through Thursday.
- Same-Day Urban Courier:
  Available in select metropolitan regions (NYC, LA, Chicago, SF, Austin) for $14.99 on orders placed before 11:00 AM.

2. ORDER PROCESSING & DISPATCH
- Orders placed Monday through Friday before 2:00 PM EST are processed and handed over to carriers on the same business day.
- Orders placed on weekends or federal holidays are processed on the next business day.
- Once dispatched, customers receive an automated SMS and email containing the tracking number and carrier link (FedEx, UPS, or DHL).

3. INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING & CUSTOMS
- We ship to over 85 countries worldwide via DHL Express Worldwide (4-7 business days).
- Customs duties and import taxes are calculated and collected at checkout (DDP - Delivered Duty Paid) so there are no unexpected fees upon delivery.

4. DAMAGED, LOST, OR STOLEN PACKAGES
- If your tracking status shows "Delivered" but you have not received your package, please check with household members and neighbors, then wait 24 hours as carriers occasionally mark deliveries prematurely.
- If still unlocated after 24 hours, contact ShopMate Support or report it to the ShopMate AI agent to initiate an immediate carrier trace or zero-cost replacement dispatch.
