================================================================================
SHOPMATE RETAIL - RETURN AND REFUND POLICY (REVISION 2026.1)
================================================================================

1. STANDARD RETURN WINDOW
ShopMate offers a 30-day standard return window from the date of package delivery for most brand-new, unopened, and gently used items. Items must be returned with original packaging, accessories, manuals, and proof of purchase.

2. CATEGORY-SPECIFIC RETURN POLICIES
- Consumer Electronics (Laptops, Smartphones, Audio, Tablets):
  Electronics may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Opened electronics are subject to a 10% restocking fee unless the item is verified defective upon arrival. All personal data and accounts (iCloud, Google, etc.) must be signed out prior to return.
- Apparel, Footwear, & Accessories:
  Eligible for 45-day returns. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have original manufacturer tags attached. Footwear must be returned in the original shoe box placed within an outer shipping carton.
- Perishables, Health, & Beauty Products:
  For hygiene reasons, opened beauty, skincare, and perishable items are non-returnable unless defective or expired at time of receipt.
- Clearance and Final Sale Items:
  Items marked "Final Sale" or discounted at 50% or more are non-returnable and non-refundable.

3. REFUND PROCESSING AND METHODS
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days after our fulfillment center receives and inspects the return.
- Credit Card refunds may take an additional 2-4 business days to reflect on your banking statement.
- Store Credit: Customers choosing store credit receive an instant digital gift card with a bonus 5% credit booster.
- Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a ShopMate fulfillment error or a defective item.

4. INITIATING A RETURN
- Returns can be initiated through the ShopMate Assistant or via your online account dashboard.
- Free pre-paid return shipping labels (FedEx/UPS) are provided for all eligible domestic returns.
- Drop-offs are accepted at any authorized FedEx Drop-off location or ShopMate partner retail hub.
