================================================================================
SHOPMATE RETAIL - PRICE MATCH & PROMOTIONAL COUPON POLICY
================================================================================

1. PRICE MATCH GUARANTEE
ShopMate guarantees the lowest price on all identical in-stock merchandise.
- Eligible Competitors: Amazon (ships/sold by Amazon directly), Best Buy, Target, Walmart, and Apple Store.
- Price Match Window: At time of purchase or within 14 days after purchase.
- Requirements: The competitor's product must be the exact same brand, model year, color, and SKU, and currently in stock on their official website.
- Exclusions: Marketplace third-party sellers, auction sites, clearance sales, doorbusters, and coupon-stacked pricing.

2. PROMOTIONAL COUPON CODES & VOUCHERS
- Only one promotional coupon code can be applied per order unless explicitly stated in multi-voucher campaign promotions.
- Active Standard Coupons:
  * 'SAVE20': 20% off all orders with minimum subtotal of $50. Max discount $100.
  * 'FREESHIP': Free expedited 2-day shipping on orders over $35.
  * 'VIP10': 10% off storewide for registered members with no minimum purchase requirement.
  * 'TECH50': $50 flat discount on premium audio and laptops with cart value above $300.
- Coupons cannot be applied retroactively to orders that have already been shipped or completed.
- Coupons cannot be redeemed for cash or transferred to another account.
